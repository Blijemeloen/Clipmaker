ClipMaker

A personal desktop tool that turns my own long videos into short vertical clips with captions.

What it does

  • Cuts my own videos into short, vertical clips (9:16) and adds captions.
  • Suggests a description and hashtags for each clip.
  • Lets me choose which clips I want and posts them to my own TikTok account using the official TikTok Content Posting API, after I log in with TikTok and give permission.

Who it is for

ClipMaker is a private tool that runs on my own computer, for my own TikTok account. It is not sold or offered to the public.
