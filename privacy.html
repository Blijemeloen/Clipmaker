Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

1. Summary

ClipMaker runs on the user's own computer. The developer does not run a server, does not collect personal data, and does not sell or share data with anyone.

2. Data from TikTok

When you log in with TikTok, ClipMaker receives an access token and your basic profile information (display name, username and avatar), and the posting options for your account. This is used only to show which account you are logged in with and to post the clips you select. The token is stored locally on your computer and is never sent to the developer or any third party other than TikTok.

3. Your videos

Your videos and clips are processed and stored locally on your computer. A clip is only sent to TikTok when you choose to post it.

4. Third-party services

  • TikTok: used to log in and to post clips you choose (official TikTok APIs).
  • Anthropic (Claude): the transcript text of a video, or still frames from it, may be sent to Anthropic's Claude AI to pick the best moments and write descriptions and hashtags.
  • Microsoft Edge text-to-speech: for videos without speech, the voice-over text may be sent to Microsoft's speech service to generate audio.

5. Deleting your data

Log out in ClipMaker to delete the stored TikTok token, or remove the app from your TikTok account settings. Deleting the ClipMaker folder removes all locally stored data.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated. The latest version is always published on this page.
